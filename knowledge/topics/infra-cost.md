## Current best understanding

**Cost control is still mostly guesswork (as of Nov 2026).** The only signal on this page so far is that measuring the impact of AI spend is the least mature part of the stack (2026-11-08). Some organisations are experimenting with using AI itself to score the value and intent of work items, so that token budgets and compute go to the highest-value tasks first.

For Parag's agentic UX and developer-portal work, this points to a design surface. Agents and portals may soon need to show users why a task got more or fewer resources, along with cost and value indicators. That would extend intent-driven design into budgeting and prioritisation. The page has no concrete data yet on serving, latency, caching, pricing or observability tooling.
